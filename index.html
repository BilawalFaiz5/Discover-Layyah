ABOUT LAYYAH

Welcome to Layyah

Layyah is one of the most beautiful districts of South Punjab. It is famous for its agriculture, peaceful environment, rich culture, historical importance and hardworking people.

This website presents tourism, history, education, industries, statistics, culture and future development of Layyah through a modern digital experience.
